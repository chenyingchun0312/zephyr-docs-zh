详细描述

Texas Instruments TMAG5273 低功耗线性 3D 霍尔效应传感器

SENSOR_ATTR_FULL_SCALE on SENSOR_CHAN_MAGN_XYZ is expressed in G, like the magnetometer channels themselves, e.g. 400 G or 800 G on a TMAG5273A1.
